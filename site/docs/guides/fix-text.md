# Fix text with a share link

This page is for you if a developer sent you a link to fix or translate the text of a website. You need no account, no password and nothing to install. You fix the text on the real site and send your changes back when you are done.

## Open the link

Open the link in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or a phone. If the site has its own login, sign in as usual.

The site opens inside the editor. On your first visit you see three tips and a field for your name. The name is optional; it tells your developer who made the changes. The tips show only on your first visit, so here is what they say. You can come back to this page at any time.

- Pick a text: hold Alt (Option on Mac) and click it, or tap it on a phone.
- Type your fix. The page changes as you type, and your changes are kept in this browser.
- When you are done, press **Send to developer** and paste the link to your developer.

At the top you see which language you are editing, for example "Editing: Arabic". The editor guesses it from the page. If it is wrong, pick the right one in the **Language** menu. When you click a text that looks like another language, the editor offers to switch for you.

## Pick a text

There are two ways to pick a text on a computer:

- Hold **Alt** (Option on Mac) and click the text.
- Or turn the switch above the page to **Edit** and just click the text. In Edit, links and buttons on the site do not react, so a click picks their text. Turn the switch to **Browse** to use the site's menus and links again, for example to go to another page.

You can also pick a text from the **Texts** list beside the page. Its search box finds a text by its words.

The editor opens with the text you picked. Above the field you see the original text, labelled with its language, for example "English". When you edit that original language itself, it says "Before your change" instead. The small **Text ID** line is the name your developer uses for this text; you can ignore it.

Some texts cannot be picked. They are not set up for editing yet, so ask your developer about them.

## Fix it and save

Type your fix in the field. The page shows it as you type.

Press **Save** (or Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on Mac). Save keeps your change in this browser. It does not change the live site and does not tell anyone yet. Your saved changes stay when you reload the page or close the tab.

A counter at the top shows how many changes you have not sent, for example "5 changes not sent". **Changes** lists them all. **Undo** puts the published text back.

If you leave a text without saving, the editor asks whether to keep editing or discard your change.

## Find what is still missing

When you translate, you want to know what is left. Three things help:

- **Coverage** at the top shows how much of the site is translated. Click it to see only the missing texts.
- **Missing** in the **Texts** list shows every text your language still needs, on every page, with a count, for example "Missing · 12". Missing texts also carry a "missing" mark in the list.
- **Next missing**, beside Save, jumps to the next text without a translation: first on this page, then anywhere on the site. When nothing is left it says "Every text is translated."

A missing text starts with an empty field. The original text is shown above it, so you can translate it in place.

## Numbers and placeholders

Some texts change with a number, like "1 item" and "5 items". For these the editor shows one field per form instead of one field. Each field says which numbers it is for, for example "for 3–10, 103–110, 203–210, …" in Arabic. Fill every field marked "needed for Arabic" (or your language). The **#** sign stands for the number; keep it where the number should go. **Edit raw** shows the whole text in one field, if you prefer.

Some texts hold a word in curly braces, like {name} in "Hello, {name}". This is a placeholder: the site puts something in its place, such as a person's name or a price. Keep each placeholder exactly as it is, braces included. You may move it within the sentence, but do not translate or delete it. The editor shows the placeholders above the field and marks one in red when your text has lost it.

If you save while a placeholder is missing, the editor explains the problem. Choose **Keep editing** to fix it. **Save anyway** also works; it marks the change so your developer checks it.

## Send to developer

When you are done, press **Send to developer**. You can add a note for your developer. Then:

- **Copy link**, and paste the link to your developer in chat or email.
- **Download file**, for a large batch. Send the file to your developer.
- **Share link…** on a phone opens the phone's share menu, so you can send the link by any app.

One link usually holds all your changes. If the editor says only some of them are in this link, send again for the rest. If it says the link is too long for a chat message, use Download file instead.

## After you send

Your developer opens the link, checks your changes on the real page and receives them as a pull request to approve. Once they approve, your changes go live on the site.

In **Changes**, each change shows where it stands:

- **Not sent**: saved in this browser only.
- **Sent**: you handed over the link or file. Nothing on the site has changed yet.
- **Live now**: your change is on the site.
- **Changed by someone else, please check**: the text on the site changed after you edited it. Look at it again.

Your changes live in this browser only. Another browser or computer does not see them, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Send them before you stop for the day. To keep a copy, or to carry on from another computer, use **Download backup** in Changes, then **Restore backup** on the other computer.

## On a phone

On a phone the site takes almost the whole screen.

- The switch at the bottom picks what a tap does. In **Edit**, tap any outlined text to fix it. In **Browse**, the site works as usual, so you can open menus and go to other pages.
- The editor slides up from the bottom. **Save** and **Next missing** are under the field.
- The bar at the top shows your language and **Send · 3**, the number of changes not sent. Tap it to send them.
- The **⋯** menu has the page address, the **Texts** list and **Changes**.

A phone forgets saved changes more easily than a computer. A private tab loses them when it closes, and some phones clear the data of sites you have not opened for a while. Send your changes as soon as you are done.
